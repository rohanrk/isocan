# 14 October 2026 — A pick is the next brief

## Exploration: the loop that nudges itself

Rohan asked for something "a little silly": wireframes and design options on
the canvas, a flow where he picks one, and the pick triggering the next round
— without him pinging the agent every time. Was that isocan's job, or the
agent's, with isocan just a canvas?

**The finding was that almost all of it already existed.** `/wire` composes
wireframes with variations; `/variation` fans any screen into siblings that
carry `parent`; `prefer` holds the eye test; `choose` and the item menu's
**Choose this variation** fold the winner home as one undo; `wait --item`
parks an agent on a screen. What did not exist was the handoff.
[atlas/convergence.md](../projects/atlas/convergence.md) had recorded it in
as many words since September — *"No decision post. `choose` does not write
the decision to the parent's thread."* So an agent parked on the source woke
on a bare `item.addVersion` and could not tell "go this way" from "I edited
it"; an agent parked on the Chat heard nothing; and the person closed the gap
by typing to the agent, which is the ping.

**The split is one sentence**, and it is the same one the design competition
settled on: *isocan owns the turn, the agent owns the content.* Nothing here
counts rounds, sets fidelity or decides how many options there are — that is
policy, and the agent (or `/wire`'s composer) runs it. What isocan owns is
that the pending decision is visible, the pick is one gesture and one undo,
and the pick lands as words the waiting agent hears.

**Phase 1, built today: the decision is said.** `convergePlan` now carries a
`post` and `convergeOps` sends it last in the fold's group, from both doors:
a comment in the chooser's voice on the source's thread — the newest one
pinned there, or a new one — reading *Chose "B" over "A", "C".*, naming the
authors of the options (the `createdBy` of each sibling's current version,
the chooser left out), with `Next: …` when `isocan choose --then "…"` said
what comes next. Three decisions worth keeping:

- **On the source's thread, never the Chat.** The first draft put it in the
  Chat because "that is where the agent is"; that wakes every agent on the
  canvas for one screen's decision. Mentioning the options' authors recovers
  the wake (`reasonFor`: "mentioned") without the broadcast, and `wait --item
  <source>` hears it anyway because a reply touches its thread's anchor.
- **Last in the group**, so an agent it wakes reads a canvas where the fold
  already happened — and one ⌘Z takes the version, the trash and the words
  back together, because `thread.create` and `thread.reply` invert.
- **No new op**, for the reason `converge.ts` already gave: the vocabulary
  can say this.

The guide's design topic gained "The exploration lap": park on the source,
wake on `Chose`, a bare pick means *this direction*, `Next:` is the brief,
`Next: done` means stop, and always make the next round from the source
rather than the trashed winner. The project is
[docs/projects/exploration/](../projects/exploration/journey.md) — six
scenes, the mechanism, and the walk. Phase 2 is the pick bar in the web
(flip = `prefer`, **Keep this** with a *Next:* field, **None — try again**);
phase 3 is the end-to-end run with a real harness, the first of which is
Rohan's own.
